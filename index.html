01 — The problem

Your EHR was built for everyone. Not for diabetes.

General-purpose EHRs treat a 30-year diabetic the same as a sprained ankle. The result: hours of duplicated charting, manual CGM data entry, and clinical context lost between visits.

01

Documentation burden

Endocrinologists spend 2+ hours per day on charting. Generic templates force you to re-type the same diabetes context every visit — insulin regimen, CGM trends, hypo events.

02

CGM data lives elsewhere

Dexcom Clarity, LibreView, Tidepool — each in its own portal. You toggle between four tools to see one patient's last 14 days. Most data goes unreviewed.

03

No diabetes-native intelligence

Your EHR doesn't know what an A1C of 7.8 with rising time-above-range means for medication titration. You hold all the pattern recognition in your head.

04 — FAQ

Questions endos ask.

Does diabetes.care replace my EHR?

No — and that's the point. We sit alongside your existing EHR (athenahealth, Epic, eClinicalWorks, Tebra, others) and handle the diabetes-specific workflows your EHR doesn't. You keep your billing, claims, and primary chart. We handle CGM review, AI scribing, A1C trending, and diabetes-specific patient management.

Is the platform HIPAA compliant?

Yes. All infrastructure is HIPAA-compliant from day one. We have BAAs with every vendor handling PHI: Supabase (database & auth), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic & OpenAI (AI), Postmark (email), Twilio (SMS), and Stripe (payments). SOC 2 Type 1 audit underway.

How does the AI scribe work?

You record the visit (live or upload audio after). Within 60 seconds, our system transcribes it (via Whisper) and generates a structured SOAP note (via Claude) using diabetes-specific clinical templates. You review and edit before signing. The note auto-pulls patient context — A1C, CGM trends, medications — from their chart.

Which CGMs are supported?

Dexcom G6/G7 at launch via the Dexcom API. Abbott Libre 2/3 and Medtronic Guardian arriving in v1.1 (Q1 2027). Tidepool integration provides legacy device coverage in the meantime.

What's the beta program?

We're admitting 10 endocrinology practices to a private beta starting Q1 2027. Beta practices get 60 days free, direct access to the founder, and lock-in pricing of $299/provider/mo regardless of practice size. In exchange, we ask for weekly feedback calls.

Do you support e-prescribing?

Not in v1. Most endocrinologists already use their main EHR for e-Rx. We're adding DoseSpot integration in v1.1 (Q2 2027) for practices that want a single workflow.

Can I migrate patient data from my current system?

We provide a structured CSV import for patient demographics, medication lists, and historical A1C values. For deeper migrations (clinical notes, attachments), we'll work with you 1:1 during onboarding.
