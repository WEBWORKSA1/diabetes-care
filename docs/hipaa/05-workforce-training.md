# 05. Workforce Training Outline

Every workforce member must complete training:
- **At hire** (before granted access to any PHI).
- **Annually** thereafter.
- **Ad-hoc** after material policy change.

Documented sign-off required. Solo founders still document this for themselves; OCR doesn't care that you're solo, they care that you have records.

## Required training topics

### Module 1 — Privacy basics (30 min)
- What is PHI? 18 identifiers.
- HIPAA's three rules: Privacy, Security, Breach Notification.
- Minimum necessary standard.
- Patient rights (access, amendment, accounting, restriction, confidential communications).
- TPO (Treatment, Payment, Operations) vs. authorization-required uses.

### Module 2 — Security awareness (45 min)
- Strong passwords + MFA.
- Phishing recognition with examples.
- Social engineering scenarios.
- Workstation security: lock screen, no PHI on personal devices.
- Safe email and messaging: never use non-BAA tools for PHI.
- Mobile device usage.

### Module 3 — Tool-specific (30 min)
- diabetes.care app: how to use audit log, how to flag suspicious access.
- AI Scribe: when not to use it (sensitive conversations the patient hasn't consented to record).
- Patient portal: link delivery, what we tell patients about emergencies.
- Lab results release: provider judgment on holding results.

### Module 4 — Breach awareness (20 min)
- What constitutes a breach.
- The 24-hour internal notification rule (see breach protocol).
- Do not delete or alter anything when a breach is suspected.
- Who to call first (Privacy Officer, then HIPAA support service).

- I am responsible for protecting the privacy and security of all PHI I access.
- I will only access PHI necessary for my job function.
- I will report any suspected breach immediately to the Privacy Officer.
- Failure to follow these rules may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
