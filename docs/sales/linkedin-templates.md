# LinkedIn outreach templates

LinkedIn is slower than email but more personal. Best when sequenced with email — send an email, then connect on LinkedIn 2-3 days later with a soft note.

## Connection request (no message)

Use for high-quality prospects you want in your network. Generic connection requests with no note convert about 30% on LinkedIn for physicians.

## Connection request with note (300 chars max)

```
Hi Dr. {FirstName} — building an endocrinology-specific EHR with Dexcom integration + AI scribe. Saw your practice focuses on diabetes; would love to connect and learn what's working / not working in your current EHR. No pitch.
```

## After they accept (Day 1)

Wait 24 hours. Don't pitch immediately.

```
Thanks for connecting! Real quick — if I told you 80% of an endo's charting could be auto-drafted by an AI scribe trained on diabetes workflows specifically, would that be interesting enough for a 15-min look?
```

## After they reply (positive)

```
Great — let me send you my Calendly: {link}

Or just hit me with 3 times that work next week. I'll keep it to 15 minutes and show you a live charting workflow (no slides).
```

## After they reply (skeptical "how are you different")

```
Fair question. The honest version:

• We're 1 person, not 50. No implementation team, no 6-month contract.
• We don't try to be everything. We're a diabetes wedge — endo follow-ups, GLP-1 visits, CGM reviews.
• The AI scribe is the differentiator. Source-linked SOAP drafts, dual LLM (Claude + GPT-4o), 60 seconds.

Worst case you watch a 5-min demo and move on. {Calendly link}
```

## After they post relevant content (engagement-based)

When a target endo posts about EHR pain points, GLP-1, CGM, or AI in healthcare:

```
{Genuine comment on the post itself, then DM:}

Saw your post on {topic}. I'm building exactly that solution — not a feature on top of Athena, a purpose-built endocrinology EHR. Would you mind if I showed you what we've built? Curious if it matches what you're hoping to see.
```

Engagement-based outreach has the highest conversion rate because you've already shown you care about their content.

## Don't do this

- Don't paste your pitch deck. They won't read it.
- Don't send InMail unless you have Sales Navigator and the prospect is unconnectable.
- Don't follow up more than 3 times after no response. Move on.
- Don't connect, immediately pitch, then disconnect when they say no. Bad form, small community.
- Don't send the same message you sent via email — they'll see right through it.
