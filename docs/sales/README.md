# Sales playbook

For selling diabetes.care to endocrinology practices. Bootstrap, founder-led, B2B SaaS. Internal use only.

## Who you sell to

**Target buyer profile:**
- US-based endocrinologist running a solo, 2-5, or 6-15 provider practice
- Currently on Athena, eClinicalWorks, Practice Fusion, AdvancedMD, or worse
- Frustrated with EHR documentation burden
- 50%+ of patient panel is diabetes
- Sees ≈30–50 patients/week

**Who NOT to call yet:**
- Hospital-employed endos (they can't change EHRs)
- Practices already on Epic / Cerner (will not switch for a wedge product)
- 20+ provider groups (procurement cycle is 6–18 months)
- Anyone outside the US (regulatory + Dexcom partnership only US for now)

## Total addressable market (pilot phase)

~7,000 US endocrinologists. ~3,500 in independent practices. Filter to "diabetes-heavy" practices = ~2,000 realistic targets. You need 200 customers to hit $1M ARR. That's 10% penetration. Achievable.

## How to find them

### Free / cheap
- **NPI Registry** (npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov) — search Specialty: "207RE0101X" (Endocrinology). Free.
- **Doximity** — if you have a clinician contact, ask them to make warm intros. Best-converting source.
- **State medical board directories** — most states have searchable databases of licensed endos.
- **AACE physician finder** (aace.com) — American Association of Clinical Endocrinology member directory.
- **Local diabetes association events** — ADA Scientific Sessions, regional AACE meetings.

### Paid
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — best ROI. Filter: Title contains "Endocrinologist" OR "Endocrine", Company Size 2-200, US.
- **ZoomInfo / Apollo** — cheaper but lower quality data for healthcare.
- **Definitive Healthcare** — if you raise money later. Too expensive for bootstrap.

## Cadence

**The locked plan in user memory mentions a 30-prospect Sales Nav target.** Aim for:
- 30 prospects identified per week
- 1 attempt = 1 email + 1 LinkedIn message + 1 follow-up email after 4 days
- Expect 5–8% reply rate cold (much higher with warm intro)
- Of replies, 30–50% take a discovery call
- Of discovery calls, 20–40% become trials
- Of trials, 30–50% become paying customers

Math: 30 prospects/week → ~2 replies → ~0.5 calls → ~0.15 trials → 0.05 customers. **About 1 customer per 4 weeks at the bottom of the funnel.**

To hit 10 customers in 90 days, scale prospect volume to ~80/week, or get better at any step. The biggest unlock is warm intros — ask every existing connection.
