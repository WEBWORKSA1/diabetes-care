# Pilot Agreement Template

One-page agreement for the first 5-10 paying customers. Use this AFTER the discovery + demo, when they say "yes, let's pilot." Have your attorney review before using with a real customer.

---

## Pilot Agreement — diabetes.care

**Effective Date:** _______________
**Practice:** _______________ ("Practice")
**diabetes.care:** _______________ LLC ("Provider")

### 1. Scope of pilot

Provider grants Practice access to the diabetes.care software platform for a 30-day pilot period beginning on the Effective Date. Practice may use the platform with up to {N} of its providers and an unlimited number of patients.

### 2. Pricing during pilot

The first 30 days are provided at no charge. Beginning on day 31, Practice will be billed at the Pilot Rate of 50% off standard pricing for 6 months, after which standard pricing applies. Standard pricing is:

- **Per-provider base:** $399 (1 provider), $299 (2-5), $249 (6+) per month
- **AI Scribe add-on:** $99 per provider per month
- **Patient Engagement add-on:** $149 per provider per month

Practice will be billed monthly to the credit card on file. The pilot rate begins month 2.

### 3. Data

3.1. Practice retains all rights to all Protected Health Information (PHI) entered into the platform.

3.2. Provider will execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Practice before any real PHI is entered into the platform. The BAA is attached as Exhibit A.

3.3. At termination of this agreement, Practice may export all of its data in standard formats (CSV, FHIR JSON) at no charge. Provider will retain the data for 30 days post-termination, then delete it permanently.

### 4. Support

4.1. Provider will respond to support requests during the pilot period within 1 business day.

4.2. Support is provided via email to: _______________

4.3. The pilot includes a 1-hour onboarding video call within the first week.

### 5. Limitations of liability

5.1. THE PLATFORM IS PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND.

5.2. Provider's total liability for any claim arising from this agreement is limited to the fees paid by Practice to Provider in the 12 months preceding the claim.

5.3. Provider is not liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages.

5.4. **Clinical responsibility:** The platform is a tool, not a clinical decision-maker. Practice's clinicians retain full responsibility for all clinical decisions, including those informed by data, suggestions, or output produced by the platform.

### 6. AI scribe disclosure

6.1. The AI scribe processes audio recordings to draft clinical documentation. Practice acknowledges that:

  (a) AI-generated drafts may contain errors or omissions ("hallucinations").
  (b) Clinicians must review and approve all AI-generated content before signing.
  (c) Patients should be informed if their visit is being recorded for AI scribe purposes, in accordance with state law and Practice policy.

6.2. Provider uses Anthropic and OpenAI as AI subprocessors. Both have signed BAAs with Provider and operate under Zero Data Retention terms. No PHI is used to train any AI model.

### 7. Termination

7.1. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.

7.2. If Practice terminates within the first 30 days (pilot period), no fees are owed.

7.3. Upon termination, Provider will assist Practice in exporting all data within 14 days.

### 8. Confidentiality

Each party agrees not to disclose the other's confidential information except as required by law or as needed to fulfill this agreement.

### 9. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of {State}. Disputes will be resolved in {State} courts.

### 10. Entire agreement

This document plus the attached BAA (Exhibit A) constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

## Notes for the founder (not part of the agreement)

- This is a template, not legal advice. Have an attorney review it. $500-$1,500 one-time.
- The BAA (Exhibit A) is critical. Use a standard template from your HIPAA support service.
- Keep the agreement to 1 page if possible. Endos won't sign 5-page contracts at the pilot stage.
- Sign electronically (DocuSign, HelloSign, Anvil) — don't make them print and scan.
- Save signed copies in a dedicated folder. You'll need them for compliance audits later.
