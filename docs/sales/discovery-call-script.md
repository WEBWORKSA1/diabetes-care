# Discovery call script (30 minutes)

The goal of the discovery call is **not** to sell. It's to:

1. Confirm they're a fit (qualification)
2. Learn what they actually need (so the demo lands)
3. Earn the right to a follow-up demo

If you do all three, you'll close ~30% of qualified discovery calls into trials.

## Pre-call prep (5 min)

Before the call:
- Look up the practice on NPI Registry + their website
- Note: their current EHR (sometimes on website footer)
- Note: how many providers, location, sub-specialties
- Read their bio for anything specific (research interests, board roles)

## Opening (2 min)

```
Dr. {LastName}, thanks for the time. I'll be quick — here's how I'd love to use the next 30 minutes:

First 20 minutes: I want to ask you about your current charting workflow and what's working / not working. I genuinely want to learn, even if we never work together.

Last 10 minutes: I'll show you what I've built. You tell me if it would solve any of what you just described, or not.

Sound fair?
```

This frames it as discovery, not pitch. Most endos relax immediately.

## The 7 questions

Ask these in order. Don't skip ahead. Take notes.

### 1. "What EHR are you on today?"

Follow-ups:
- How long?
- Are you on contract / when does it renew?
- Did you choose it or inherit it?

Why: tells you switching cost + timing.

### 2. "Walk me through a typical T2DM follow-up visit from when the patient walks in to when you sign the note."

Let them talk. Don't interrupt. This is gold.

Why: reveals the real workflow. Where is documentation time spent? Where are the screen-clicks? What do they hate?

### 3. "Roughly how much time do you spend on documentation per visit? Per day?"

Follow-ups:
- After-hours ("pajama time")?
- Do you finish notes the same day or do they pile up?
- Have you tried Nuance / Suki / Abridge / Freed?

Why: quantifies the AI scribe value prop. Average endo: 2-3 hours/day on documentation.

### 4. "How do you currently handle CGM data?"

Follow-ups:
- Do patients send screenshots?
- Do you log into Clarity / LibreView during the visit?
- Time spent per CGM review?

Why: CGM-in-chart is a major differentiator. Endos hate portal-hopping.

### 5. "What about GLP-1 visits — how do you manage starts, titrations, side effects?"

Follow-ups:
- Are GLP-1 visits a growing share of your panel?
- How do you track who's on what dose?

Why: GLP-1s are 30%+ of endo visits and growing. We have purpose-built workflows.

### 6. "What about your front-office — scheduling, reminders, no-shows, intake forms?"

Follow-ups:
- Who handles scheduling?
- What's your no-show rate?
- Do you send appointment reminders? How?

Why: SMS reminders + patient portal is half the product value but rarely the buying trigger. Bring it up so they know it exists.

### 7. "If you could change one thing about your current EHR, what would it be?"

Follow-ups:
- Why hasn't that changed yet?
- Have you looked at alternatives recently?

Why: their #1 pain point becomes your demo focus.

## Qualification (after the 7 questions, ask yourself):

- [ ] Independent practice (1-15 providers)?
- [ ] At least 50% diabetes panel?
- [ ] US-based?
- [ ] EHR contract renewing in next 12 months OR strong dissatisfaction with current?
- [ ] Decision-maker on the call (or warm path to one)?
- [ ] Will allocate time to trial a new tool?

5/6 → strong fit, push for demo + trial.
3-4/6 → keep warm, follow up in 6 months.
0-2/6 → polite close, focus elsewhere.

## Demo (10 minutes)

See `demo-walkthrough.md`. Tailor it to what they told you in the discovery. If they hated CGM portal-hopping, lead with CGM-in-chart. If they hated documentation time, lead with AI scribe.

Do NOT show every feature. Show 2-3, the ones they actually care about.

## Close (3 min)

```
Dr. {LastName}, what you described is exactly what I built this for. Two options for next steps:

Option 1: I send you a private trial environment — your own demo practice with 30 simulated patients. You poke around for a week, see if the workflow feels right.

Option 2: If you want to skip ahead, we set up a 30-day free pilot with 5-10 of your real patients. No credit card, no contract.

Which is closer to what you'd want to do?
```

If they hesitate: "What would need to be true for you to try this?"

That question — "what would need to be true" — is the single highest-conversion question in B2B sales. Use it.

## After the call

Same day, send a recap email:

```
Subject: Recap + next step — {Practice name}

Dr. {LastName},

Quick recap of what I heard today:

• You're on {EHR}, contract renews {date}
• Biggest pain: {their top complaint}
• Roughly {X hours/day} on documentation
• GLP-1 share of panel: {%}
• Already use Dexcom for {Y%} of patients

If I got any of that wrong, let me know.

Next step: {whatever you agreed on}. I'll send the trial link by {date}.

— {Your name}
```

The recap shows you listened. The deadline creates accountability.
